💡 Legal Terms & Procedures

🚀 Required Next Steps

  1. Verify Challan Authenticity: Log on to the official Parivahan portal at echallan.parivahan.gov.in and enter your challan number.
  2. Compounding / Online Payment: Settle the fine amount within 60 days via UPI, Net Banking or Card to close the offence before it is referred to court.
  3. Contesting or Virtual Court: If wrongly issued or contesting the charge, file a dispute online or wait for referral to the Virtual Court at vcourts.gov.in.
