# Driving Licence: Learner's Licence, Validity and Renewal
Source: Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 (as amended in 2019): Sections 3, 4, 8, 9, 14, 15; Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 (Forms 1, 1A, 2, 9; Rule 32 fees); Sarathi portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.

## Minimum age for a licence (Section 4)
A person aged 16 can get a licence for a motorcycle with engine capacity up to 50cc, with the consent of a parent or guardian. A person must be 18 to drive any other motor vehicle (such as a car or a geared motorcycle). The minimum age for driving a transport (commercial) vehicle is 20 years.

## Learner's licence
A learner's licence (LL) is needed before applying for a permanent driving licence. It is valid for six months. You can apply for the permanent driving licence at least 30 days after the learner's licence is issued, and before it expires. Apply on the Sarathi portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) using Form 2, with proof of age and address, a passport photograph and a self-declaration of physical fitness (Form 1). Many States allow the learner's licence test to be taken online from home with Aadhaar-based authentication. While learning, an "L" sign must be shown on the vehicle, and for a car a person holding a valid licence must sit beside the learner.

## Validity of a driving licence (Section 14)
A driving licence for a non-transport (private) vehicle is valid:
- if issued before age 30: until the holder turns 40;
- if issued between ages 30 and 50: for 10 years;
- if issued between ages 50 and 55: until the holder turns 60;
- if issued after age 55: for 5 years.
A licence for a transport (commercial) vehicle is valid for 5 years. A licence endorsed for vehicles carrying hazardous goods is valid for 3 years. The expiry date is printed on the licence and can be checked on the Parivahan or Sarathi portal.

## When to renew a driving licence (Section 15)
You can apply for renewal up to one year before the licence expires. If you apply more than one year after the licence expired, the licensing authority must refuse to renew it unless you pass the driving test of competence again (Section 15(4)), and a higher renewal fee applies. If you are 40 or older, or renewing a transport licence, the application must include a medical certificate. Since the 2019 amendment, you can renew a licence at any licensing authority (RTO) in India, not only the one that issued it. Driving with an expired licence can be treated as driving without a valid licence (Section 181, fine Rs 5,000), so renew before the expiry date.

## How to renew a driving licence online
1. Go to sarathi.parivahan.gov.in and select your State.
2. Choose "Driving Licence" > "Services on DL (Renewal/Duplicate/AEDL/IDP/Others)".
3. Enter your driving licence number and date of birth, check the details, and select "Renewal of DL".
4. Fill in Form 9 (application for renewal) and upload your photograph, signature and documents.
5. Upload a medical certificate in Form 1A, signed by a registered medical practitioner, if you are 40 or older or are renewing a transport licence.
6. Pay the fee online and book a slot at the RTO if your State requires biometrics or verification in person.
7. The renewed licence (smart card) is sent by post, and the digital version can be downloaded from DigiLocker or mParivahan.

## Documents needed for licence renewal
- The existing or expired driving licence
- Application in Form 9
- Medical certificate in Form 1A (if 40 or older, or for a transport licence)
- Recent passport-size photograph and signature
- Proof of address and age (such as Aadhaar), if the details have changed
- Fee receipt

## Licence fees (Central Motor Vehicles Rules, Rule 32)
The central fee schedule (revised in 2016) includes: learner's licence Rs 150 plus Rs 50 for the test; driving test Rs 300; issue of the driving licence Rs 200; renewal Rs 200; and, for late renewal after the grace period, an extra Rs 1,000 for each year or part of a year of delay. International Driving Permit: Rs 1,000. Smart-card and service charges vary by State, so check the final amount on the Sarathi portal before paying.

## Duplicate licence and change of address
If a licence is lost or damaged, apply for a duplicate on the Sarathi portal under "Services on DL" by selecting "Issue of Duplicate DL". Some States ask for a police report (or an online lost-document report) for a lost licence. To change the address on a licence, use the same service and select "Change of Address in DL", uploading proof of the new address.

## International Driving Permit
An Indian licence holder who plans to drive abroad can apply for an International Driving Permit (IDP) through the Sarathi portal under "Services on DL". It needs a valid Indian driving licence, a passport, a visa (where applicable) and a medical certificate. An IDP is valid for one year or until the Indian licence expires, whichever is earlier.
