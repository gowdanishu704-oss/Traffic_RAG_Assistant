# Tamil Nadu: Compounding Fines for Traffic Offences (Section 200 Notification)
State: Tamil Nadu
Source: Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary No. 491, Chennai, 19 October 2022, Part II, Section 2. Home Department, G.O. Ms. No. 758, Home (Transport-VII), 19th October 2022, Notification No. II(2)/HO/811(a)/2022, issued under Section 200(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. It supersedes Notification No. II(2)/HO/519/2011 and is in force from 20 October 2022. Signed by K. Phanindra Reddy, Additional Chief Secretary to Government. Transcribed from the gazette copy hosted at simpliance.in.

## Tamil Nadu: what a compounding fine is and who can collect it
Under Section 200(1) of the Motor Vehicles Act, the Tamil Nadu Government lets offences be compounded, meaning settled by paying a fixed amount (an on-the-spot fine) instead of facing prosecution, either before or after prosecution starts. G.O. Ms. No. 758 (in force from 20-10-2022) authorises:
1. All Police Officers not below the rank of Special Sub-Inspector of Police, including Traffic Police, across the State except at Transport Department check posts. They can compound all sections in the table except Sections 178(1), 178(2), 178(3)(a) and 178(3)(b), 182(2), 182A(1), 182A(3) and 182A(4), 192A(1), 194(1), 194(1A), 194(2) and 194A.
2. Motor Vehicles Inspectors (Non-Technical) at Transport Department check posts. They can compound all sections except 182A(4), 183(1)(i) and 183(1)(ii), 184, 194B(1) and 194B(2), 194C and 194D.
3. Transport Department officers not below the rank of Motor Vehicles Inspector (Grade-II), across the State except at check posts. They can compound all sections in the table.
Drunk driving (Section 185) is not in the Tamil Nadu table, so it cannot be compounded and goes to court.

## Tamil Nadu fine for not wearing a helmet, seat belt, triple riding (Sections 194B, 194C, 194D)
In Tamil Nadu, the compounding fine is:
- riding a motorcycle without a protective headgear (helmet), for the rider or pillion rider (Section 194D): Rs 1,000;
- carrying more persons on a motorcycle than its capacity, such as triple riding (Section 194C): Rs 1,000;
- driving without a safety belt (seat belt), or carrying passengers not wearing seat belts (Section 194B(1)): Rs 1,000;
- driving with a child under 14 years not secured by a safety belt or child restraint system (Section 194B(2)): Rs 1,000.

## Tamil Nadu fine for overspeeding (Section 183)
Driving at excessive speed, against the speed limits under Section 112, in Tamil Nadu:
- light motor vehicle (car), under Section 183(1)(i): Rs 1,000;
- medium or heavy motor vehicle (goods and passengers), under Section 183(1)(ii): Rs 2,000.

## Tamil Nadu fine for dangerous driving, jumping a red light and using a mobile phone while driving (Section 184)
Driving dangerously (rash and negligent driving), including using handheld communication devices (a mobile phone) while driving, under Section 184 in Tamil Nadu: Rs 1,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
(Note from the Act, not from the gazette: the Explanation to Section 184 of the Motor Vehicles Act says that jumping a red light, violating a stop sign, using handheld communication devices while driving, passing or overtaking unlawfully, and driving against the authorised flow of traffic all count as dangerous driving.)

## Tamil Nadu fine for driving without a licence or allowing an unlicensed person to drive (Sections 180, 181, 182)
- Driving without a driving licence, against Section 3 or Section 4 (Section 181): Rs 5,000.
- Allowing unauthorised persons to drive any motor vehicle (Section 180): Rs 5,000.
- Driving while disqualified from holding a licence, or getting a licence without disclosing previous endorsements (Section 182(1)): Rs 10,000.
- Similar offences relating to a conductor's licence (Section 182(2)): Rs 10,000.

## Tamil Nadu fine for driving without insurance, registration or permit (Sections 192, 192A, 196)
- Driving an uninsured vehicle, against Section 146 (Section 196): Rs 2,000 for the first offence; Rs 4,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Using a motor vehicle without registration (Section 192(1)): Rs 2,500 for the first offence; Rs 5,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Driving a transport vehicle without a permit or against permit conditions (Section 192A(1)): Rs 10,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.

## Tamil Nadu fine for pollution, noise and road safety standards, no PUC (Section 190(2))
Driving, or allowing a motor vehicle to be driven, in a public place in violation of standards for road safety or control of air or noise pollution (for example, no valid PUC or excess emissions), under Section 190(2) in Tamil Nadu: Rs 10,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.

## Tamil Nadu fine for horns, emergency vehicles, racing and unfit driving (Sections 186, 189, 194E, 194F)
- Blowing the horn unnecessarily or in prohibited areas, or using an exhaust cut-out that bypasses the silencer (Section 194F): Rs 1,000 for the first offence; Rs 2,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Failing to pull to the side of the road for an ambulance, fire service vehicle or other emergency vehicle (Section 194E): Rs 10,000.
- Racing or trials of speed on a public road without permission (Section 189): Rs 5,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Driving when mentally or physically unfit to drive (Section 186): Rs 1,000 for the first offence; Rs 2,000 for the second and subsequent offence.

## Tamil Nadu fine for overloading and excess passengers (Sections 194, 194A)
- Exceeding the permissible weight, against Sections 113, 114 and 115 (Section 194(1)): Rs 20,000, plus Rs 2,000 per tonne of excess load.
- Refusing to stop the vehicle or submit it for weighing, or removing excess load before weighing (Section 194(2)): Rs 40,000.
- A load extending beyond the sides, front, rear or permissible height (Section 194(1A)): Rs 20,000.
- Carrying excess passengers in a transport vehicle beyond the permitted seating capacity (Section 194A): Rs 200 per excess passenger.

## Tamil Nadu fine for general offences, traffic signs, documents and registration lapses (Section 177)
Under Section 177 (general offences with no specific penalty), the Tamil Nadu compounding fine is Rs 500 for the first offence and Rs 1,500 for the second and subsequent offence. It applies to each of these:
- violating mandatory traffic signs;
- failing to produce the driving licence, conductor's licence, registration certificate, permit, fitness certificate or insurance certificate on demand;
- failing to renew the registration of a motor vehicle;
- failing to apply for re-registration within 12 months of bringing a vehicle from another State;
- the seller (transferor) or buyer (transferee) failing to report a transfer of ownership within the prescribed period;
- wilfully removing, altering, defacing or tampering with traffic signs;
- leaving a motor vehicle in a dangerous position in a public place;
- carrying a person on the running board, or otherwise outside the body of the vehicle; travelling on the running board or on top of the bonnet;
- allowing a passenger to stand, sit or place anything so that it hampers the driver's control;
- leaving a vehicle stationary without a licensed driver in the driver's seat or without a stopping mechanism;
- failing to stop at an unguarded railway level crossing to make sure no train or trolley is coming.

## Tamil Nadu fine for disobeying orders, ticketless travel and other offences (Sections 178, 179, 182A, 182B, 198)
- Disobeying orders of the authority, obstructing its duty, or refusing to give information (Section 179(1)): Rs 2,000. Giving false information to the authority (Section 179(2)): Rs 2,000.
- Travelling in a stage carriage (bus) without a proper pass or ticket (Section 178(1)): Rs 500.
- A bus conductor (or driver acting as conductor) failing to accept the fare, failing to issue a ticket after collecting the fare, or issuing an invalid or lower-value ticket (Section 178(2)): Rs 500.
- Refusing to ply a contract carriage or carry passengers on a two-wheeled or three-wheeled vehicle, such as an auto-rickshaw (Section 178(3)(a)): Rs 50. Refusing to ply or carry passengers in any other case (Section 178(3)(b)): Rs 500.
- Unauthorised interference with a motor vehicle, such as entering it or tampering with its mechanism (Section 198): Rs 1,000.
- A manufacturer, importer or dealer selling or altering a vehicle against Chapter VII (Section 182A(1)): Rs 1 lakh per vehicle. Selling a non-compliant critical safety component (Section 182A(3)): Rs 1 lakh per component. An owner altering or retrofitting a vehicle in a way not permitted (Section 182A(4)): Rs 5,000 per alteration.
- Contravening Section 62A (Section 182B): Rs 5,000.
