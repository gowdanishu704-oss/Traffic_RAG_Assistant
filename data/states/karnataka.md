# Karnataka: Compounding Fines for Traffic Offences (Section 200 Notification)
State: Karnataka
Source: Karnataka Gazette (Rajyapatra), Part IVA, No. 781, Bengaluru, 21 September 2019. Transport Secretariat Notification No. TD 250 TDO 2019, dated 21-09-2019, issued under Section 200 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 and Section 21 of the General Clauses Act, 1897. It supersedes the earlier Notification No. TD 250 TDO 2019 dated 03-09-2019. Signed by K. Beeresh, Deputy Secretary to Government, Transport Department. ## Karnataka: what a compounding fine is and who can collect it
Under Section 200 of the Motor Vehicles Act, the State Government can let certain offences be compounded. This means the offender pays a fixed amount (an on-the-spot fine) to an authorised officer instead of being prosecuted in court. The Karnataka notification of 21-09-2019 authorises two groups of officers to collect the amounts listed in its table:
(1) all officers of and above the rank of Motor Vehicles Inspector of the Transport Department;
(2) officers of and above the rank of Assistant Sub-Inspector of Police (Traffic), and the Sub-Inspector of Police in charge of a police station, within their jurisdiction. Police officers cannot compound offences under Sections 113, 114, 115 read with Section 194 (overloading); Section 182; Section 62A read with Section 182B; Sections 182A(1), 182A(3), 182A(4), 190, 192, 192A, 194 and 194A. Only Transport Department officers can compound these.
The notification came into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette (21 September 2019). Offences that are not in the table, such as drunk driving (Section 185) and jumping a red light under Section 184, are not compoundable under this notification and go to court.

## Karnataka fine for not wearing a helmet, seat belt, triple riding (Sections 194B, 194C, 194D)
In Karnataka, the compounding fine is:
- not wearing a protective headgear (helmet) on a two-wheeler (Section 194D): Rs 500;
- violating safety measures for motorcycle drivers and pillion riders, such as triple riding (Section 194C): Rs 500;
- not using safety belts (seat belts) and child seating rules (Section 194B): Rs 500.
(The central Act prescribes Rs 1,000 for each of these; the Karnataka compounding amount is Rs 500.)

## Karnataka fine for overspeeding (Section 183)
Driving at excessive speed in Karnataka (Section 112 read with Section 183):
- two-wheeler, three-wheeler or light motor vehicle (car), under Section 183(1)(i): Rs 1,000;
- medium goods or passenger vehicles, heavy goods or passenger vehicles and others, under Section 183(1)(ii): Rs 2,000.

## Karnataka fine for using a mobile phone while driving (Section 184(c))
For dangerous driving, the Karnataka notification compounds only the use of handheld communication devices (mobile phones) while driving, under Section 184(c):
- first offence: two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,500; light motor vehicle (car) Rs 3,000; other vehicles Rs 5,000;
- second and subsequent offence: Rs 10,000.
Other forms of dangerous driving under Section 184, such as jumping a red light, violating a stop sign, wrong-side driving and unlawful overtaking, are not listed in the notification.

## Karnataka fine for driving without a licence or allowing an unlicensed person to drive (Sections 180, 181, 182)
- Driving without a valid licence, or below the minimum age (Sections 3 and 4 read with Section 181): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,000; light motor vehicle (car) Rs 2,000; other vehicles Rs 5,000.
- Owner allowing an unauthorised (unlicensed or underage) person to drive (Sections 3 and 4 read with Section 180): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,000; light motor vehicle Rs 2,000; other vehicles Rs 5,000.
- Driving during a period of disqualification (Sections 19 and 20 read with Section 182(1)): Rs 10,000.

## Karnataka fine for driving without insurance or registration (Sections 192, 196)
- Driving an uninsured vehicle (Section 146 read with Section 196): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,000; light motor vehicle (car) Rs 2,000; heavy goods vehicle and others Rs 4,000.
- Using a vehicle without registration (Section 39 read with Section 192(1)): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 2,000; light motor vehicle Rs 3,000; medium or heavy goods or passenger vehicles and others Rs 5,000.
- Using a vehicle without a permit or violating permit conditions (Section 66(1) read with Section 192A): Rs 5,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.

## Karnataka fine for pollution, noise and unsafe vehicles, no PUC (Section 190(2))
Under Section 190(2), Karnataka compounds:
- using a vehicle in unsafe condition: two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,500; light motor vehicle, heavy vehicle and others Rs 3,000;
- violating air pollution standards (for example, no valid PUC or excess emissions): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,500; light motor vehicle, heavy vehicle and others Rs 3,000;
- violating noise pollution standards: two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 1,500; light motor vehicle, heavy vehicle and others Rs 3,000.
Only Transport Department officers (Motor Vehicles Inspectors and above) can compound Section 190 offences, not the police.

## Karnataka fine for horns, emergency vehicles, racing and unfit driving (Sections 186, 189, 194E, 194F)
- Use of horns and silence zones (Section 194F): two-wheeler or three-wheeler Rs 500; other vehicles Rs 1,000.
- Not giving way to (failing to allow free passage to) an ambulance, fire engine or other emergency vehicle (Section 194E): Rs 1,000.
- Racing and trials of speed (Section 189): Rs 5,000 for the first offence; Rs 10,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Driving when mentally or physically unfit to drive (Section 186): Rs 1,000 for the first offence; Rs 2,000 for the second and subsequent offence.

## Karnataka fine for overloading and excess passengers (Sections 194, 194A)
- Driving a vehicle exceeding permissible weight (Sections 113, 114 and 115 read with Section 194): Rs 5,000 for carrying an overload, plus Rs 2,000 for every additional tonne of excess load, together with liability to pay charges for off-loading the excess load.
- Carrying excess passengers (Section 194A): Rs 200 per excess passenger.

## Karnataka fine for general offences, disobeying orders, ticketless travel and other offences (Sections 177, 178, 179, 182A, 182B, 198)
- General offences with no specific penalty (Section 177): Rs 500 for the first offence; Rs 1,000 for the second and subsequent offence.
- Disobedience of orders, obstruction and refusal of information (Section 179(1) and (2)): Rs 1,000.
- Travelling without a pass or ticket, dereliction of duty by a conductor, and refusal to ply a contract carriage (Section 124 read with Section 178(1), (2) and (3)(b)): Rs 500; under Section 178(3)(a): Rs 50.
- Unauthorised interference with a vehicle (Section 198): Rs 1,000.
- Offences relating to construction, maintenance, sale and alteration of motor vehicles and components (Chapter VII read with Section 182A): Rs 1,00,000 per motor vehicle under Section 182A(1); Rs 1,00,000 per component under Section 182A(3); Rs 5,000 per alteration under Section 182A(4).
- Contravening Section 62A (read with Section 182B): Rs 10,000.
